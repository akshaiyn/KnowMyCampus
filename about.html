About KnowMyCampus

An interactive campus discovery platform designed to help students, faculty and visitors easily explore campus information in one place.

📖 About the Project

KnowMyCampus is a web-based interactive campus discovery platform developed as a mini project. The system brings important campus information such as departments, facilities and locations together in a single platform.

Navigating a large educational campus can be difficult for new students, visitors and other users. Information about departments, laboratories, facilities and important locations may be distributed across different sources.

KnowMyCampus aims to provide a simple, user-friendly and interactive solution. Users can search for campus information, explore academic departments, view facilities, access the campus map and interact with an AI-based assistant.

Problem & Solution

❓

Problem Statement

Students and visitors may face difficulty finding departments, facilities, laboratories and important locations within a large campus. Information may not be available through a single, easy-to-use platform.

💡

Proposed Solution

KnowMyCampus provides a centralized interactive website where users can search campus information, explore departments and facilities, locate important places and obtain assistance through an AI-based interface.

🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of KnowMyCampus are:

1

To provide centralized campus information through a single web platform.

2

To help students and visitors easily find departments and facilities.

3

To provide an interactive campus map for locating important places.

4

To integrate an AI assistant for answering campus-related questions.

5

To develop a simple, accessible and user-friendly campus navigation system.
